Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The Short Version

Ei is local-first. Your data stays on your machine. I receive none of it. There's nothing to request access to, nothing to delete from my servers, and nothing I could hand over even if someone asked me to.

What Data Is Collected

When you connect Slack (or other integrations), Ei reads your messages, channel history, and user profiles via OAuth on your device. That data is processed locally and stored in $EI_DATA_PATH on your machine. None of it is transmitted to me.

Ei may also receive transient data as part of normal API interactions — for example, user IDs in OAuth payloads. This data is not logged or stored by the developer.

The One Exception: Sync

If you explicitly enable the optional Sync feature, your Ei data is encrypted on your device before it leaves. The encryption key is derived from your username and passphrase and never transmitted. I store an encrypted blob I cannot read, even if I wanted to. Sync exists only to let you move between devices. It is off by default.

How Data Is Used

Locally: to power Ei's features on your device. Full stop.
Synced: to let you restore your local Ei state on another device. That's it.

How Long Data Is Kept

Local data lives on your device until you delete it or clear $EI_DATA_PATH. Synced (encrypted) data on my servers is retained until you request deletion.

Access, Transfer, and Deletion

Local data: delete $EI_DATA_PATH and it's gone.
Synced data: email me and I'll delete it. It's an encrypted blob — I can't read it, but I can remove it.
